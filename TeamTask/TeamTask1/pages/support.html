Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I track my order?

Open the Orders section and select your current order to view its status.


How can I cancel my order?

Open your order and check whether the cancellation option is available.


My payment failed. What should I do?

Check your payment method and try again. If money was deducted, contact support.


How can I get a refund?

Select the relevant order and contact support regarding the refund.


My order is delayed. What should I do?

Check the order tracking information or contact Swiggy support for assistance.
