About Our Project

About the Project

Our project focuses on understanding the daily experiences and challenges faced by food delivery riders. Delivery riders play an important role in connecting restaurants with customers.

This website provides information about rider challenges, registration, safety, support, and rider welfare.

Our Objectives

  • Understand the challenges faced by delivery riders.
  • Promote rider safety and awareness.
  • Explain the rider registration process.
  • Provide useful support information.
  • Promote better working conditions for riders.

Why Delivery Riders Matter

Delivery riders are an important part of the food delivery system. They help restaurants deliver food to customers quickly and conveniently.

  • Connect customers with restaurants.
  • Provide convenient food delivery.
  • Support restaurants and businesses.
  • Create flexible earning opportunities.

Rider Welfare

Rider welfare is important for creating a safe and supportive working environment. Riders may face traffic, bad weather, vehicle maintenance costs, and road safety risks.

  • Road safety
  • Health and rest
  • Emergency support
  • Financial awareness
  • Better communication

Our Vision

Our vision is to create awareness about the important role delivery riders play in our daily lives and encourage a safer, more supportive, and respectful environment for them.
